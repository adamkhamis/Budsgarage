Our Story

From Mobile Wrench to Full Shop

Bud's Garage is the next chapter of a mobile mechanic and tire business built on honest work and fair prices.

Built on the Road, Grounded on Trinity Street

Before Bud's Garage had four walls and a hoist, it had a van full of tools and a reputation for showing up, doing the job right, and never overcharging. That reputation is why the shop exists today.

How We Got Here

Our Journey

Bud's Garage started as a mobile mechanic and tire service, driving directly to Vancouver customers for repairs, tire changes and maintenance wherever they needed it — driveways, workplaces, and roadsides across the city.

As demand grew, so did the need for a home base: a proper shop with lifts, equipment and space to take on bigger jobs that can't be done curbside. That's what opened the doors at 3689 Trinity Street.

Today, Bud's Garage operates as both — a full-service shop for repairs big and small, and the same trusted mobile mechanic and tire service that built the business in the first place. One team, two ways to get the job done.
